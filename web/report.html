Absolute and relative accuracy are different figures

Absolute position is bounded by the GNSS receiver — 1–3 m without RTK, and no processing recovers what the receiver never knew. Relative measurement is far tighter, because scale error enters as a single global multiplier rather than per-point noise. Measurement tasks depend on the relative figure. Both are reported; conflating them overstates the system.

Method note

Why attitude fusion is not optional

Failure mode

A straight pass produces a near-collinear GNSS track. Collinear points cannot constrain rotation about the flight axis, so roll is left free.

The failure is silent. Scale converges to under 0.5 % error in every variant tested, so a team validating on scale alone concludes the alignment is sound — then finds every measurement wrong with no indication why.

Track collinearity0.88
Correction

Rotation is taken from IMU attitude by chordal rotation averaging; scale from the ratio of spreads about the centroid, which is rotation-invariant.

GNSS multipath near tall structures is rejected by RANSAC before the fit. Three fixes were discarded on this pass.

Depth uncertainty follows σZ ≈ Z²⁄(f·B)·σd — quadratic in range, inverse in baseline. Consecutive frames intersect at 1.68°, which is numerically indefensible. Stride 20 was adopted; stride 40 gains little further and costs correspondence reliability.

Why the larger number is a trap

Allocation past physical VRAM does not raise on Windows — the driver falls back to host memory over PCIe. The run continues and step time collapses from 150 ms to 888 ms.

Scoping to the number that merely avoided a crash produces a pipeline that appears to work in testing and misses its time budget under load. The ceiling is the last configuration that stays resident.

Declaration of limits

26.6 % of this scene was never observed.

Rear façades and terrain beyond the flight corridor fall outside every camera frustum on a single pass. Those regions are marked as inferred throughout the model and are excluded from every measurement in this report.

No geometry has been synthesised to fill them. A surface presented as measured was resolved from at least two independent views.
