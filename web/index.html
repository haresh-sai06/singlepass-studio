Design principle

A model that invents a wall it never saw is worse than one with a visible hole.

One pass leaves roughly a quarter of any scene unobserved. That is geometry, not a defect — and no amount of inference changes it.

Every surface is graded by how many cameras genuinely resolved it. Inferred regions are marked, never blended in silently. An analyst always knows which measurements carry weight.
